### What these catch, without pinning internals

- **Weak/strong / `*`**: assert the `ETag` header string (`W/"…"` vs `"…"`) and 304 vs 200. A refactor that weakens strong tags, or that treats `*` as a literal ETag, flips those rows. Do not assert `response.weak_etag?` unless you already do in response tests; the header is the contract.
- **Precedence**: default AND vs `strict_freshness` XOR. A merge of the two modes, or an RFC-7232-only rewrite, fails 6 or 8 without anyone reading `fresh?`’s branches.
- **Subsecond**: asserting the truncated `Last-Modified` **and** 304 on that IMS fails a refactor that compares the original `Time` or that emits ISO8601 with fractional seconds.
- **Method**: 304 on POST is today’s controller contract. Dropping it “because RFC” fails row 10. HEAD 304 with a blank body is the same `head :not_modified` path as GET; do not also demand Rack HEAD semantics.
- **Cache-Control / `no_store`**: compare the committed header. That covers default injection, `public: true` skipping the default, and last-writer `no_store` without mentioning `handle_conditional_get!`.
- **Template ETags**: equality across two requests, inequality after `modify_template`, inequality across `template:` values. Never assert digest hex or `etaggers`.
- **Block execution**: `@ran` is the user-visible `stale?` contract (skip expensive work on 304). Status 304 with `@ran == true` (work ran, then 304) or 200 with `@ran == false` is semantic drift even if headers look right.

Extra class-level `etag { }` values belong in the existing [`TestControllerWithExtraEtags`](file:///home/user/workspace/repo/actionpack/test/controller/render_test.rb#L8-L42) tests; do not re-encode them in this matrix.

Do not add assertions on call order, double `fresh?` evaluation, or private helpers. If a refactor still produces the same status, body, validators, `Cache-Control`, and `stale?` side effects on these rows, the public HTTP contract held.
