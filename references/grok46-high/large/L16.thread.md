- create: `remember_transaction_record_state` sets `@_new_record_before_last_commit` from the snapshot
- update: `_update_record` sets `@_trigger_update_callback`
- destroy: `@_trigger_destroy_callback ||= persisted? && destroy_row > 0`

| Final state | Fires | Does not fire |
