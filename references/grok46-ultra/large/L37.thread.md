If the product goal is “allow inspect/mutate then `deliver_later`,” that needs a new delivery path: serialize the generated email (e.g. encoded RFC822), teach `MailDeliveryJob` to deliver that object instead of re-calling the mailer action, and decide what happens to `deliver` callbacks, `rescue_from`, parameterized `params`, and request/I18n state. Tests would need mutation survival, parameterized processed enqueue, `deliver_later!`, and serialization errors. That is a feature, not a one-line deletion. Workarounds remain those in the current error: don’t touch the message, mutate only inside the mailer action, or enqueue a custom job that owns the `Mail::Message`.
