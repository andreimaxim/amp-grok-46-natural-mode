Also: reading `config.autoload_paths` during configure then `config.paths.add(...)` still registers (existing #49629 test). That must keep working — it is the reason `all_*` must stay live concatenations until the engine initializers run.

### How to avoid order-only coupling

- Replace `assert_equal existing_autoload_paths, main.dirs` as a **preservation gate** with:
  - `assert_equal existing_autoload_paths.to_set, main.dirs.to_set`
  - plus one shadowing probe if two roots define `Foo`: assert which `Foo` wins **if** current Zeitwerk order is considered user-visible. If the refactor may reorder engine `unshift`, document that the winner may change and **do not** fail the suite on winner unless product says first-registered engine wins.
- For once extras `e1,e2,e3`, assert each `∈ once.dirs` and `∉ main.dirs` and that `E1,E2,E3` autoload; drop `slice(e1_index, length)`.
- Prefer `assert_includes` / set difference empty over `assert_equal` on full `$LOAD_PATH` or `Dependencies.autoload_paths`.
- Callback tests: increment counters; do not inspect `__callbacks` order.
- Do not assert `@_all_autoload_paths` identity, `uniq` vs `Set` implementation, or `reverse_each` on `$LOAD_PATH` beyond “membership + uniqueness”.
